Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: 21 October 2026

AQ Setu respects requests to delete personal information associated with the AQ Setu Client Portal and connected integrations. You can submit a deletion request using the process below.

How to request deletion

  1. Contact your AQ Setu account administrator or the AQ Setu support contact provided to you for the portal.
  2. State that you are requesting deletion of data from the AQ Setu Client Portal and identify the account or integration concerned (for example, the Facebook/Instagram account connected to your client workspace).
  3. For security, we may ask you to verify that you control the account or are authorized to make the request. Do not send passwords, access tokens, or other login credentials.

What happens next

We will review and process verified requests within the period required by applicable law. Where deletion is appropriate, we will delete or de-identify the requested personal data from active systems, subject to legal, security, accounting, dispute-resolution, and legitimate business-record retention requirements. Some backups may persist for a limited period before routine rotation.

Meta / Facebook / Instagram data

You can also remove AQ Setu Client Portal from your Facebook account's Business Integrations settings to revoke its access. Revoking access and requesting deletion are separate actions: if you want data already stored by AQ Setu deleted, please submit a request using the process above as well. Disconnecting the integration inside the portal may stop future access but does not necessarily delete historical campaign reports or records.

Questions

If you need help identifying the right account or submitting a request, contact your AQ Setu account administrator or the AQ Setu support contact provided to you for the portal.
