Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

AQ Setu (“we”, “us” or “our”) operates the AQ Setu Client Portal, a platform that helps authorized clients view and manage digital marketing information and connected advertising and social media services. This policy explains what information may be processed when you use the portal or connect an account.

Information we process

  • Account and profile information: information needed to authenticate you and associate your login with the appropriate client account.
  • Marketing and reporting data: campaign metrics, advertising account identifiers, leads, reports, and related information made available through enabled integrations.
  • Connected Meta account data: when you authorize a Meta integration, we may process the permissions, account/page identifiers, insights, campaign data, and other data necessary to provide the features you approved.
  • Technical information: basic request, security, and diagnostic information used to operate, protect, and troubleshoot the service.

How we use information

We use information to authenticate users, provide client dashboards and reporting, operate integrations requested by an authorized user, maintain security, troubleshoot issues, and meet applicable legal obligations. We do not state or imply that connecting an account grants permissions beyond those shown during authorization.

How information is shared

Information may be accessible to authorized AQ Setu personnel and the client users who have permission to access the relevant account. Data is also processed by service providers needed to host the portal, store its data, or provide an integration (for example, Meta or Google when those services are connected). We do not sell personal information.

Retention and security

We retain information for as long as needed to provide the service, maintain appropriate business records, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. We use access controls and reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect stored information; no online service can guarantee absolute security.

Disconnecting an integration

You can disconnect an available integration from the portal where that feature is provided. Disconnecting stops the portal from using the saved connection for future integration requests. Historical reports or records may remain where needed for client reporting, recordkeeping, or legal purposes. You can also remove the app from your Meta Business Integrations settings to revoke its access on Meta's side.

Your choices and data requests

You may request access, correction, or deletion of personal information associated with your use of the portal, subject to applicable law and legitimate retention requirements. See our Data Deletion Instructions for how to submit a request.

Children's privacy

The portal is a business service and is not intended for children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the portal or its practices change. The current version will be published at this URL with its latest revision date.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact your AQ Setu account administrator or the AQ Setu support contact provided to you for the portal. Please do not include passwords or access tokens in your request.
